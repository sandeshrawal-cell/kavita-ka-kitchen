# Version 3.2.2 — Audit #4 follow-up

- Public recipe cards now expose a canonical `/recipe/<dish>` URL while open, with a share action. Closing the dialog returns to the prior app location. Private family recipes are never given public links.
- Large recipes show measured ingredients per cooking batch and explicit conversions for numeric quantities in the method. Cooking mode repeats the batch guidance; times and doneness checks are applied per batch. A pilot batch and vessel check are still necessary for production-scale cooking.
- Office, factory and catering menus can be exported as a self-contained, printable menu proposal. The client quote is optional and uses the entered price per guest; internal ingredient estimates are omitted. The operator must review pricing, service, taxes and terms before sharing it.
- The local kitchen assistant's example now returns real matches. Empty searches display the exact failure and optional, user-triggered ways to relax time, cuisine or category. Dietary exclusions are never relaxed by those controls.
- `Guest Menus` now contains 104 guest-friendly dishes from existing complete recipes. The app explains these are dishes to combine into a menu. No duplicate dish records were added. Fresh Lime Soda and a burrito bowl were corrected to their actual categories.
- Home inspiration selection remains rotating. Non-home inspiration is drawn from guest-friendly or bulk-suitable dishes.

## Still requiring operator input or editorial production

The public Terms and Privacy notice still need the operator's publishable name, contact, jurisdiction and review of applicable launch terms. We have requested those facts; the app must not invent them. Public email signup also remains paused until a verified sender and SMTP delivery exist, with email confirmation kept on. Cloud AI remains disabled at the owner's request. The small drink, soup, salad, bake and leftover categories still need more distinct, complete recipes with reviewed realistic images and English, Gujarati and Hindi copy; moving existing dishes into a filter does not create new culinary variety.
